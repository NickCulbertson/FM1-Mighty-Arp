Mighty Arp

Mighty Arp is custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. It's a 4-op FM and subtractive hybrid synth built around a chance arpeggiator. You choose the notes, the arp decides when they play. Hold a chord, set CHANCE, and let it create melodies. Plus it has a 32-step sequencer, 64 sounds, chords with strum, swing and humanize, a chorus, a ping-pong delay and a reverb with shimmer, 16 presets of your own, and MIDI and clock in and out.

Version 1.0. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

  • Try it in the browser: the same code the FM-1 runs, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
  • Install: from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
  • Manual: playing, the pages, presets, MIDI. One page.
  • Download mightyarp-1.0.fwsc: for the command-line installer in fm1-base, python3 tools/fm1_install.py mightyarp-1.0.fwsc.
  • Source: MPL-2.0. Built on fm1-base by Charles Vestal and felucca-bsp, from Felucca by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments.
